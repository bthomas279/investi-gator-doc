Detect AI, bots, and scams
while scrolling.

Investi-gator is a Chrome extension that passively reads posts on social media platforms as they load, badging posts that contain AI-generated images and text, are likely bot accounts, and/or are scams without you lifting a finger.

See what it detects → Watch the demo

What it detects

Four layers of scrutiny on every post

As posts enter your feed, Investi-gator runs multiple light local detectors in the background for each post, generating badges for any post that exibits any of the detection types, along with confidence and reasoning.

🖼️

AI-generated images

Analyzes images attached to posts and flags those signatures of generative models.

✍️

AI-generated text

Scores post text for machine-written patterns, helping you tell authentic voices from synthetic ones.

🤖

Likely bot accounts

Weighs account signals to estimate whether the poster is an automated or inauthentic account.

🎣

Probable scams

Recognizes scam, phishing, and spam patterns in posts and comments so you can avoid them before engaging.

Zero effort

Detection that happens as you scroll

There's no button to press and no post to paste. Investi-gator watches your feed passively, evaluating each post the moment it appears and quietly tagging the ones worth a second look — so protection is the default, not an extra step.

Under the hood

How it works

1

Observe

A content script watches the social media feed and reads new posts as they render passively.

2

Local Analysis

Each post's text and media are passed to the local models for analysis where they return it's classification and confidence for each type.

3

Cloud Analysis

If a local model give a low enough confidence score for a detection type, a heavier cloud model for that type is called to classify the post.

4

Generate

Any positive detection that is allowed by the user's personal settings and Investi-gator's requirements are badged and displayed along with the confidence

5

Reason

When you click on a badge, the content scripts requests the reasoning for the positive detection for each type. This can range from LIME, SHAP, Grad-CAM, or heatmaps depending on the detection type.
