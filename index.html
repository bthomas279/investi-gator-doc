Investi-gator is a Chrome extension that passively inspects social media posts as they load — flagging AI-generated images and text, likely bot accounts, and probable scams without you lifting a finger.

What it detects

Four layers of scrutiny on every post

As posts enter your feed, Investi-gator runs a set of lightweight detectors in the background and surfaces a clear, unobtrusive verdict on anything that looks off.

🖼️

AI-generated images

Analyzes images attached to posts and flags those that show the tell-tale signatures of generative models.

✍️

AI-generated text

Scores post text for machine-written patterns, helping you tell authentic voices from synthetic ones.

🤖

Likely bot accounts

Weighs account signals to estimate whether the poster is an automated or inauthentic account.

🎣

Probable scams

Recognizes scam patterns in posts and comments so you can steer clear before you ever engage.

Zero effort

Detection that happens as you scroll

There's no button to press and no post to paste. Investi-gator watches your feed passively, evaluating each post the moment it appears and quietly tagging the ones worth a second look — so protection is the default, not an extra step.

How it works

1

Observe

A content script watches the feed and picks up new posts as they render, without interrupting your browsing.

2

Analyze

Each post's images, text, and account details are passed to the detectors for a quick verdict.

3

Flag

Anything suspicious gets an inline badge, keeping you informed while you keep scrolling.
