Try the Models

Paste some text below and Investi-gator's local scam (V1.5) and AI text (V2) detectors will classify it like they would in the extension. This also includes the reasoning behind each detection as well. There's no usage limits either so feel free to analyze as much as you want.

  • I'm still optimizing and improving both the AI-text and scam detector. They WILL sometimes get things wrong.
  • I recommend a 80 character mimimum input if you want to maximize accuracy. You could classify just the letter "h" if you really wanted to, but that wouldn't be very informative would it?
  • Maximum text input is 1000 characters. The models properly process long series of text like research papers. They're more-so meant for social media posts, or shorter forms of text.
  • Nothing you paste is uploaded, stored, or sent anywhere. None of what you paste or analyze will be used for further fine-tuning either.
  • Bot and AI-image detection models have not been developed yet. Only AI-text and scam detection are currently available. I will periodically update this page when improved versions of the models are pushed.
  • If you have any feedback or critques regarding the models, don't hesitate to connect and message me on LinkedIn. The process of fine-tuning LLMs is still new and complicated for me, and I'd love to recieve any advice or recommendations you may have.
